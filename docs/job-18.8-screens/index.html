What the screenshots show

The after window is wider, so the bubbles use up to 88% of the chat column and more text fits per line. Pictures and display math stay centred and keep their limits. The child’s text is the design ink (#15103a in Night sky) on every subject colour; Job188ChatTests checks WCAG contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for all six subjects in both themes. The scroll policy and the “New message ↓” pill are covered by the headless tests, because the transparent Luna window cannot be captured by DWM (the Job 18.4 limitation).
